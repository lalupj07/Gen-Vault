# GenVault

## Register
product

## Platform
adaptive

## Users
People who need offline TOTP codes and passwords on phone and desktop — unlock, copy, lock.

## Purpose
Zero-knowledge offline authentication vault: master-password-gated credentials and 2FA without cloud sync.

## Positioning
The most secure offline vault that still feels calm and fast on mobile.

## Personality
secure · calm · precise

## Visual lane
GenVault dark security tokens. Soft purple accent for primary actions only. Figma-inspired layout (header, hero summary, sectioned lists, bottom nav + FAB) — not a pastel todo aesthetic.

## Anti-references
Playful 3D onboarding kits, cream/sand AI defaults, decorative glassmorphism, marketing card grids, emoji-as-icons.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast, ≥44px touch targets, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion`.
